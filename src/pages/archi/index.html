Made By Me

Archi

A small, handmade jewelry and home goods brand I run in my kitchen. This isn't a store — just where I share what I'm making.

Archi grew out of a part-time jewelry business I ran for four years called WhitMIMade. It's grown into polymer clay jewelry, laser-cut pieces, and small-batch resin home goods, all made by hand, in small runs, out of my own kitchen.

I don't want Whit's World to turn into an Archi storefront — so this page is just an honest look at what I'm currently making. If something catches your eye, everything lives on Etsy.
